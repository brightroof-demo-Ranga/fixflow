From: Nirosha Perera, Head of Operations, BrightRoof Solar
Subject: Need a system for service calls

Hi,

We have about 2,000 customers with rooftop systems and 12 technicians.

When a system stops working, customers call our hotline or message us on WhatsApp. Our coordinator writes it in a Google Sheet and calls a technician.

Problems:

- Calls get lost, especially on weekends
- Customers keep calling to ask "when is someone coming?"
- We don't know how long jobs take or which technicians are overloaded
- Our warranty contract says we must respond to critical faults within 24 hours and we can't prove we do

We'd like something simple where customers can log a problem, we can assign it, technicians can update it from their phones, and management can see what's going on. Photos would help a lot.

Ideally live before the December rush. Budget is limited, so start small.
